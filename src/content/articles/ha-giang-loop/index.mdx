  <BookText>
    The Ha Giang loop was originally pioneered by and created for motorcycle tourers, we chose to ride it on our bicycles. The loop traverses around the Dong Van Karst Plateau, a collection of steep limestone canyons and peaks in the northernmost corner of Vietnam. The route is well known for its small and dangerous mountain roads, giving you incredible views into the green valleys below.
  </BookText>

  <BookText heading="The journey to Ha Giang">
    Getting there was one of the biggest travel nightmares of my life. A visa error at Noi Bai sent me to Bangkok on the first flight out. Three days, barely any sleep, and a dubious payment later, I was finally let into Vietnam.

    After a happy reunion and a night in Ha Noi, we assembled our boxed-up bikes in the rain and caught the bus deep into the mountains.
  </BookText>

  <BookText heading="Village Nights">
    Even the tiny mountain villages we travelled through had a bustling night life. Markets and restaurants were busy late into the night with uncles sharing drinks and food. Pool rooms were also a common occurrence even on the most remote stretches of road. Our favorite thing to order when we went out was Bia hơi, a light homebrewed beer that is usually made fresh daily.   
  </BookText>
