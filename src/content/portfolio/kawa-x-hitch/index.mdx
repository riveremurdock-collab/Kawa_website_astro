The Hitch X Kawa product line was a collaboration between my bag company and Hitch Bicycle Club in South Korea. We worked together to finalize designs and colors, and I made the patterns and sewed the bags in my workshop. The collection used a mix of recycled and natural technical materials and includes a selection of bike bags based off of the bags I created for myself to tour across South Korea and Japan. They were sold in their South Korea location as well as other bike shops across southeast Asia. You can see the full collection [here](https://hitch-official.com/product/list.html?cate_no=70).
