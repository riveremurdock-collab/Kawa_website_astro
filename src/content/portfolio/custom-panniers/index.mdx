Waxed canvas touring panniers designed and sewn by myself. These were made to match the aesthetic of my bike while also being durable and secure for all terrain use. They were inspired by classic horsepacking equipment in both their style and materials. Heavy waxed canvas is durable, repairable, and far more sustainable than using plastic synthetics. Modern construction methods and the use of some synthetic parts, and modern aluminum hardware, help to keep weight down while still using mainly natural materials.
